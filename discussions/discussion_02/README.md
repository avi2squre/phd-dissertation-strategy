## What the labels mean

Entries use explicit labels where the distinction matters:

- **User-stated requirement** - a constraint, preference, factual update, or question supplied by the user.
- **Stream 02 factual claim** - a claim presented as fact in the historical reply; it may still require verification.
- **Source-supported finding** - a claim tied to an identified source at the source's recorded reading status.
- **Stream 02 inference** - a conclusion drawn beyond what a source directly establishes.
- **Stream 02 strategic judgment** - an assessment of fit, priority, risk, or likely value.
- **Stream 02 recommendation** - an action or direction recommended at the time.
- **Hypothesis / untested proposal** - a scientific or commercial proposition that had not been evaluated.
- **Later correction or qualification** - a revision to earlier wording, assumptions, or confidence.
- **Unresolved question** - a dependency or uncertainty left open by the archive.
- **Private evidence summarized and withheld** - a status supported historically by private material that is not reproduced here.

Historical labels do not upgrade evidence status or make a claim canonical.

## Public-repository redaction policy

The public reconstruction preserves enough causal reasoning to explain why the strategy favored particular scientific and portfolio properties. It does not preserve detailed immigration tactics, employer wish lists, compensation objectives, personal anxieties, private assessments of faculty, private messages, protocol identifiers, credentials, or restricted institutional information.

The retained public formulation is:

> Internal strategic objectives favored methodological work capable of producing independently recognized research contributions, transferable technical skills, external adoption, and strong publication artifacts.

Faculty reasoning is limited to public roles, official eligibility rules, methodological fit, and the need to confirm an actual advising commitment. Private evidence is summarized only as a status transition. No PHI, enclave-derived results, screenshots, patient-level examples, sensitive counts, or restricted schemas are included.
