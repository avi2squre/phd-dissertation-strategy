# The early direction era: the drug-repurposing spine, the agentic pivot, and the first advisor search

- **Period:** 2026-03-15 to 2026-04-11 (a separate thread in this stream, predating the repository; exported 2026-07-23; archived 2026-08-28)
- **Sources:** LIT-021 (ADInt, the lab knowledge graph the user's own tool is built on), LIT-022 (DrKGC); institutional sources (graduate-school committee policy, program pages, faculty directories); a documented 2026-04-03 meeting with the research mentor; the advisor of record's written feedback on the spring fellowship report

## Digest

**Inference (stream 03, at the time; market and valuation claims from contemporaneous web searches were never verified and are deliberately not reproduced here).**

1. **Three-niche framing (2026-03-15).** The era opened with an explicitly commercial career-ceiling question. The stream proposed three research niches: AI for drug discovery and precision medicine (highest ceiling), clinical AI infrastructure and foundation models for health (fastest to market), and real-world evidence and healthcare data networks (most defensible moat), with the thesis that the PhD should be treated as a launchpad rather than a destination.
2. **Constraint injection (same day).** When the user added the specialized-visa portfolio, resistance to AI commoditization, and market-position constraints, the recommendation moved to the first niche: the argument was that clinical AI infrastructure risks commoditization by foundation-model providers, while proprietary biological insight from genomic plus clinical data is a more defensible position. The user's genomics MS background was treated as the differentiator.
3. **The agentic pivot (2026-04-03, from a documented meeting).** In the user's first substantive meeting, the research mentor suggested seriously considering agentic AI in whichever health discipline the user chose, and noted that the pace of AI progress makes the graduation-year job market hard to predict. The stream then collapsed the three niches into one position: agentic AI systems for drug discovery and clinical decision-making, with the reasoning that the durable skill is the agentic layer over a domain rather than the domain itself.
4. **The era's concrete direction.** Agentic AI plus knowledge-graph drug repurposing for Alzheimer's disease, with the research mentor's lab supplying the knowledge-graph and NLP methodology and a clinical-NLP collaborator supplying phenotyping expertise. A companion plan positioned the user's ADInt visualization work as an interpretability layer over the lab's existing knowledge graph and link-prediction stack (LIT-021, LIT-022), serving simultaneously as course project, progress-report deliverable, and lab entry point.
5. **The fellowship narrative (2026-04-07, submitted).** The on-record framing of the direction, developed with the advisor of record's guidance: initial interest in causal inference and interpretability; the technical realization that LLMs extract causal relationships from text but do not discover causal structure from raw data; therefore a refined focus on knowledge-graph-based drug repurposing. This "informed evolution" narrative is the era's most explicit statement of the drug-repurposing spine.

**The user's own methodological objection (2026-04-11, unresolved in this era).** The user observed that LLM- and KG-based methods only recover what already exists in literature and data, and asked how de novo design methods (protein structure prediction, generative molecular design) would integrate. The stream answered with a two-paradigm framing (knowledge extraction versus de novo design, with an agentic layer coordinating both) and noted the local absence of a dedicated design lab. The objection was never operationalized in this era; it resurfaced in July as the admission that repurposing had been a feasibility substitute for the user's genuine design interest (see the funnel entry).

## The first advisor search (public grounds only)

Recorded here as reasoning history; `context/advisor_landscape.md` is not edited by this entry.

- The research mentor was endorsed as the anchor for the direction, with the noted caveat that his program skews to NLP, LLMs, and knowledge graphs, so a complement in genomics or biostatistics was sought.
- An exhaustive pass over program faculty concluded that the strongest genomics complement sat outside the institute, in the College of Pharmacy; the graduate-school policy allowing university tenure-track faculty to serve on doctoral committees regardless of home department was identified as the mechanism, and such a member can satisfy the outside-field committee requirement.
- The advisor of record recommended, in writing, that the user meet program faculty before turning to external advising, and characterized the direction on record as "causal discovery from text using LLMs" with flexibility. The stream advised taking the recommended meetings while keeping the existing ranking, as a goodwill and information measure.
- A funding-compliance question was raised by the advisor of record and left unresolved in this era: whether the fellowship-funding department is comfortable supporting research output primarily benefiting another department's program. The stream's position was that the acknowledgment requirement does not settle the resource-allocation concern and the program should resolve it formally.
- Monitoring-committee selection was reasoned as relationship-building under uncertainty; the final slate was the director of graduate studies, a core faculty member already known to the user, and a surgery-affiliated candidate as backup.

## Errors and corrections preserved

- The stream claimed a documented collaboration between the research mentor and the pharmacogenomics co-advisor candidate; the user found no co-authored papers, and the stream retracted it as a name conflation with a different faculty member. The pairing argument was restated as complementarity rather than existing collaboration.
- The stream inferred that the mentor's agentic-AI suggestion responded to a paper the user had presented; the user corrected that the mentor had not seen the presentation, and the inference was withdrawn. The suggestion stands on the mentor's own judgment, which the stream noted is the stronger signal.
- The stream stated the research mentor was institute-affiliated faculty; on the user's challenge this was checked and corrected: his appointment is in the Department of Surgery's computational health sciences division, and he is not on the program's graduate-faculty listing. This strengthened, rather than weakened, the advisor of record's funding concern.
- A core-faculty genomics candidate was proposed and withdrawn after the user observed the fit was wet-lab-adjacent cancer biology rather than AI-driven work; the stream conceded it had been stretching to find an internal fit.

## What survived this era, and what did not

Survived: the mentor-as-anchor structure; the agentic-methods emphasis; the complementarity requirement for the committee; the criteria structure now codified in `context/user_goals_and_constraints.md`; the ADInt substrate as the user's demonstrated asset.
