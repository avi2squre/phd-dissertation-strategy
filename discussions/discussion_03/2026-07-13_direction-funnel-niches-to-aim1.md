## Digest

**Inference (stream 03, at the time), unless marked otherwise.**

1. **Five-niche reassessment (2026-06-05, retrieved 2026-06-10).** Ranked: multimodal agentic AI for drug repurposing and clinical decision-making; AI-powered real-world-evidence and trial intelligence; federated learning for health AI (overlapping the mentor and a program co-PI's NSF award); AI clinical documentation; causal AI for precision medicine. The attached advice was to run a primary (niche 1) plus a secondary rather than choose one. These rankings were provisional and carried the era's criteria, not fresh evidence.
2. **The filter and the gap map (2026-06-10).** A literature pass applied an explicit filter: the contribution must be AI-centered (method or architecture, not a clinical finding), agentic or cleanly agentic-extensible, and traceable to the mentor lab's program. Across fifteen consolidated papers, three recurring gaps: almost nothing was Alzheimer's-specific (treated as the user's opening); KG-method papers were components without an orchestrator while agent papers were orchestrators without a curated repurposing KG (the fusion treated as the obvious move); EHR and genomics/design coverage was thin. Candidate project shapes were clustered: A (agentic repurposing over ADInt, nearest-term), B (EHR-grounded differentiation), C (causal/mechanistic originality).
3. **The turning-point meeting, reconstructed (meeting 2026-05-29; analyzed 2026-07-06 to 07-07).** The research mentor pressed on the methodology contribution, named evidence rigor (weighting evidence by study type, recency, citation structure, corroboration) as what existing collaborations do not do, stated the lab is not a visualization lab, and offered the federated rare-disease project. The user's own conclusions, stated in the thread: the visualization tool should be the byproduct, not the project; the focus should move to the agentic reasoning process. The stream separated two ideas the user had merged under "hierarchy": evidence-quality hierarchy (the mentor's ask, the research delta) and exploration-path hierarchy (a visualization feature that LIT-016's own future work already claims). The user had read LIT-016 before the meeting and articulated the differences himself; the stream initially got this wrong from the transcript and was corrected.
4. **Build reversed to read (2026-07-06).** A two-day build sprint (a retrieve-reason-verify agent over ADInt) was planned, upgraded mid-design to be evidence-rigor-aware, then cancelled the same evening on the user's challenge: the two unread papers were the sprint's prior art, and the planned loop was, in base form, LIT-001's generate-review-revise cycle plus LIT-017's confidence-scored agentic graph construction. Building first risked demonstrating incrementality; reading first defines the delta. The delta was stated precisely: bibliometric rigor weighting, distinct from LIT-017's LLM-judged confidence. The federated-project offer was to be acted on independently ("treat it as a strong secondary, not a replacement").
5. **Paths re-ranked, decision structure split (2026-07-06 to 07-08).** Five paths (A: rigor-weighted agentic repurposing over ADInt as dissertation core; B: rare-disease clinical NLP on the funded project as near-term enabler; C: rigor methods as domain-general core, year 2; D: causal AI, year 2; E: documentation track, side publications). Recommendation: run A and B together, with an explicit falsifiable test: if the close reads show the rigor delta is thin, B becomes the better core. The user then split the decision into three parts and locked two: methodological core (NLP plus agentic methods) locked; near-term execution vehicle (the funded project and its advisor path) locked; final application domain left open.
6. **The provenance correction on the direction itself (2026-07-08, user).** The user corrected the record: drug repurposing had been chosen as a feasibility substitute because the mentor does not work in drug design; the genuine interests were drug development, causal AI, precision medicine, and EHR systems. A history audit confirmed the stream's own past labels said "repurposing" throughout, and the resolving insight was adopted: NLP and agentic reasoning are the methodological core (which is the mentor's strength), and the application domain is more flexible than previously treated. A two-paradigm integration (knowledge extraction plus de novo design, orchestrated agentically, per LIT-020's architecture) was reopened as a possibility. The user deliberately deferred the domain decision rather than deciding under deadline pressure.
7. **The narrowing directives (2026-07-09, documented).** At the lab meeting the mentor stopped the planned three-paper update, elevated it to a full seminar in the NLP/IE group, and, citing policy uncertainty around international collaboration, directed the user to focus on Aim-1-related work only. This mooted the Aim-2 privacy synthesis the stream had prepared (LIT-018's role shifted from proposed mechanism to prior art) and concentrated everything on trustworthy phenotype extraction.
8. **The spine arrives (2026-07-13, documented).** The clinical collaborator scheduled the two talks (LIT-001 on 07-23, LIT-002 on 08-06) and sent LIT-003 with the statement that the project additionally needs a context-identification step: negation, experiencer, temporality, anatomic site, uncertainty. The stream's reading: LIT-003 is the strong baseline and the gap is context identification; the two-talk arc should use LIT-003 as the spine, with LIT-001 supplying verification and LIT-002 supplying temporality and evidence tiers. The ontology-granularity observation made here (parent term chosen over child, the error class a verification loop could catch) is the seed of the July proposal entry.

**Source-supported at the time (from LIT-016 and the mentor lab's record), with the verification caveats noted in the index:** LIT-016 validates LLM output against the same lab knowledge graph structurally, with no bibliometric weighting; the lab's 2021 repurposing-by-KG-completion paper (LIT-019) predates any rigor layer. The novelty claim built on these ("rigor-weighted, temporally-aware, agentic verification over a curated disease-specific graph is nobody's paper") relied on LIT-019 being rigor-free, which was assumed from a skim recommendation and never confirmed; it is recorded here as an unverified premise, not a finding.

## Publication-vehicle reasoning of this era (2026-07-01; plan, overtaken by events)

Deadline-anchored planning was adopted: one body of summer work aimed at a poster at a Pacific Symposium session on agentic systems as base case, a machine-learning-for-health venue as the archival methods target, and the field's summit as the application home, with an explicit one-archival-home rule for dual-submission compliance and a preference for open-weight models for reproducibility. No submission had happened by the era's end; the plan is archived as reasoning, not as output.

- The user hypothesized the multilingual requirement had lapsed; checked against the collaborator's transcript and corrected (the French site handles French notes; the reduced collaboration concerned a later aim).
