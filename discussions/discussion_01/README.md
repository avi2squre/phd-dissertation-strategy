## Independence rule

Stream 01 is reconstructed from its own primary historical sources rather than from the conclusions of other discussion streams. During the dedicated reconstruction pass, substantive `discussion_02` and `discussion_03` entries were not used as evidentiary sources for Stream 01's historical reasoning.

**Qualification:** before this reconstruction began, the Stream 01 system had already been exposed in the live conversation to summaries and portions of material from Streams 02 and 03 while helping coordinate their archival workflows. Complete epistemic isolation therefore cannot be claimed. The reconstruction mitigates this by grounding Stream 01 claims in the 173-page Stream 01 export and this thread's continuation, and by not importing another stream's conclusions as Stream 01 historical reasoning.

## Evidentiary labels used

- **User-stated requirement**: a criterion, constraint, correction, or preference stated by the user.
- **Stream 01 factual claim**: a factual assertion made in the discussion.
- **Stream 01 inference**: an interpretation drawn from sources or observations.
- **Stream 01 strategic judgment**: a recommendation about research-program design or prioritization.
- **Hypothesis / untested proposal**: a proposed scientific direction not established by experiments.
- **Later correction or qualification**: a revision to earlier reasoning.
- **Unresolved question**: an issue left open for literature, faculty, or empirical resolution.

## Public-repository boundary

The archive preserves causal strategic reasoning but does not expose the private objective function in detail. Detailed immigration tactics, compensation objectives, employer wish lists, personal financial ambitions, or other private motivations are omitted. Public entries may state that internal objectives favored strong independent scholarship, reusable artifacts, transferable technical depth, external adoption, and durable impact.

Named faculty appear only when the discussion relied on public methodological expertise, advisor eligibility, or institutional role. Private judgments about personality, willingness, politics, or presumed availability are not archived.
