## Why this source matters

Stream 01 cited this study when elevating evaluation, verification, and safety from a supporting concern to a serious dissertation-family candidate. It was used to motivate the claim that static medical benchmarks can miss important failures under adaptive adversarial testing.

## What the source actually establishes at screening depth

The official article presents dynamic red-teaming as a way to address gaps in conventional benchmarking of health and medical LLMs. The study uses adaptive adversarial evaluation to probe dimensions such as robustness, hallucination, privacy, and bias.

## Project interpretation

**Historical Stream 01 inference:** if model behavior degrades under adaptive evaluation that static benchmarks do not capture, then evaluation methodology itself can become a substantive research problem rather than only a reporting step.

## What this source does NOT establish

- It does not establish that all static medical benchmarks are inadequate.
- It does not establish evaluation/safety as the optimal dissertation direction.
- It does not establish how to evaluate long-horizon autonomous agents specifically.
- It does not establish that dynamic red-teaming alone produces deployable safety guarantees.
