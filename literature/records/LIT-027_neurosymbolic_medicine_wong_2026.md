## What the source actually establishes at screening depth

The official record describes neuro-symbolic approaches as integrating data-driven inference with explicit clinical knowledge to address safety, transparency, and accountability limitations in biomedical AI. The article includes a roadmap for medical neuro-symbolic AI and discusses composite versus monolithic architectures and clinical scenarios where neuro-symbolic design may be appropriate.
