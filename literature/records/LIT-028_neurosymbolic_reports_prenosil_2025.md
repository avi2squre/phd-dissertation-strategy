## Why this source matters

Stream 01 used this paper as a concrete biomedical example of combining an LLM with a rule-based expert system to produce more deterministic and auditable clinical information extraction.

## What the source actually establishes at screening depth

The abstract reports a neuro-symbolic system connecting GPT-4 to a rule-based expert system via a semantic integration platform. Candidate facts extracted from free text are checked against medical rules to produce traceable labels. The study evaluates the approach on prostate-cancer PET/CT reports and reports stronger task performance than GPT-4 alone on the evaluated tasks, with an auditable reasoning chain.

## Project interpretation
