## What the source actually establishes at screening depth

The official abstract describes MCM (Medical Concept Mapping), a neural-symbolic agent system for mapping free-text descriptions to standardized biomedical concepts. Neural components supply contextual pattern recognition while symbolic components add structured reasoning, consistency, and interpretability.
