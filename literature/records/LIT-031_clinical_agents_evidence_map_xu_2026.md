## Why this source matters

Stream 01 used this evidence map as a translational-maturity signal when reasoning about where clinical agentic AI was being deployed and where governance, auditability, and oversight remained important.

## What the source actually establishes at screening depth

The abstract states that early agent deployments are concentrated in administrative workflows while agentic applications are expanding across the clinical patient journey. The article synthesizes evidence across diagnostics, management, and care-adjacent operations.

## Project interpretation

**Historical Stream 01 inference:** the evidence map supported the view that clinical agent deployment remained early enough that rigorous evaluation, workflow integration, auditability, and oversight were still important problems.

## What this source does NOT establish

- It does not establish that a particular agent architecture is novel.
- It does not establish market demand or commercial success.
- It does not establish that clinical agent research is unsaturated.
- It does not validate Stream 01's proposed trustworthy-autonomy framing.
