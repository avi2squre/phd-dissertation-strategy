## Indexing notes

- Use stable sequential IDs (`LIT-001`, `LIT-002`, ...).
- `Type` may include journal article, conference paper, systematic review, methodological paper, benchmark paper, preprint, perspective/roadmap, or other scholarly literature.
- `Status` must be one of `DISCOVERED`, `SCREENED`, `READ`, `VERIFIED`, or `RETIRED`.
- Do not claim that a literature search is comprehensive merely because relevant sources have been indexed here.
- When a source is used to support a novelty or saturation claim, record the exact scope of that inference in its individual source record.
- Entries LIT-001 to LIT-014 were registered on 2026-08-28 from discussion stream 03. arXiv identifiers for LIT-001 to LIT-004 were read from the papers' first pages. Journal details for LIT-006 to LIT-013 come from a bibliographic check done in that stream on 2026-08-22 (search-verified for the SCREENED entries, unverified for the DISCOVERED ones); DOIs were not recorded and should be added when verified. No `VERIFIED` status has been assigned yet, because that status requires checking the project's specific claims against the source, which has not been done as a separate step for any entry.
