## 4. N2 disagreement adjudication

### Stream 02 position

Stream 02 judged N2 materially weakened because the packet made independent adjudicated validation conditional. The frozen S03-C2 thesis states that patient-level diagnostic state and transitions must be recoverable with fidelity sufficient to reproduce expert chart-review verdicts, and Aim 3 explicitly tests deployment against the ground-truth problem.

### Stream 03 position

Stream 03 judged the packet faithful with editorial clarification, arguing that institutional adjudication was uncertain in the source feasibility analysis and therefore the `where available` language was acceptable.

### Coordinator decision

**Stream 02 is correct on the scientific-target question, while Stream 03 is correct on the resource-contingency question.**

The final packet must distinguish:

1. **Thesis requirement:** some independently adjudicated reference-standard test is required to evaluate the claim of chart-review-level diagnostic-state fidelity.
2. **Resource flexibility:** no particular rare-disease cohort, institution, collaborator, or restricted dataset is mandatory. A public, governed, prospectively adjudicated, or otherwise scientifically defensible substitute is acceptable.
3. **Failure condition:** if no defensible independent reference-standard test can be constructed or obtained, the full normalized thesis cannot be established even if benchmark/formalism work remains publishable.

This preserves both the frozen thesis and the explicit feasibility uncertainty without converting a resource contingency into a scientific-scope exemption.
