## C. N2 adjudication-specific review

**Question.** Does requiring some independently adjudicated reference-standard test for the full N2 thesis, while explicitly not requiring ciliopathy, rare disease, one institution, or one restricted cohort, preserve both the frozen thesis and the feasibility contingency without pre-selecting a dissertation application?

**Frozen thesis.** S03-C2 section 4: "Patient-level diagnostic state and its transitions are recoverable from longitudinal notes with fidelity sufficient to reproduce expert chart-review verdicts on diagnosis status; and models that represent the trajectory explicitly outperform per-mention assertion classification aggregated across notes." Falsification: "if neither approach approaches adjudication fidelity, the application claim dies." S03-C2 section 12 names clinician adjudication as "the only non-derived ground truth." Round 3 section 3 N2 lists "cohort-ground-truth validation" among S03-C2's retained contributions and bounds N2 only against becoming a representation-repair dissertation.

**Frozen feasibility contingency.** S03-C2 section 13 places clinician adjudication and governed note access under "currently uncertain"; section 11 states that failure of Aim 3 "still leaves a defensible dissertation" (formalism, benchmark, metrics, negative result). This concerns dissertation survival, not the truth of the thesis.

**Assessment of the coordinator's resolution.**

1. *Thesis preservation.* Requiring an independently adjudicated reference-standard test is what the first thesis clause needs in order to be tested at all. The Draft 0.1 wording "clinically useful agreement with an independent reference standard where such a standard is available" allowed the full thesis to be declared established without the clause ever being tested. Stream 03's audit accepted that wording as editorial by reading it against the feasibility record; the coordinator's distinction between a thesis requirement and a resource contingency is the more faithful reading, and Stream 03 concedes the upgrade to material.
2. *Contingency preservation.* The resolution keeps the contingency exactly where the frozen record puts it: no particular cohort, institution, collaborator, or restricted dataset is mandatory, and the stated failure condition ("if no defensible independent reference-standard test can be constructed or obtained, the full normalized thesis cannot be established even if benchmark/formalism work remains publishable") is S03-C2 section 11 restated with the thesis-versus-survival distinction made explicit.
3. *No pre-selection.* The resolution names no disease, condition, site, or dataset. The illustrative substitutes ("public, governed, prospectively adjudicated, or otherwise scientifically defensible") are resource-flexibility guidance, not scientific content; one editorial safeguard is recommended when implementing: the substitute must retain the property the frozen source assigns to adjudication, namely a reference standard derived independently of the fields being evaluated (S03-C2 section 12; S01-C2 section 12's evidence-release simulation with independently defined truth is compatible). The coordinator's phrase "independently adjudicated" already carries this; the implementer should keep the word "independently."

**Conclusion.** The resolution preserves the frozen thesis and the feasibility contingency and pre-selects no application. ACCEPT RECONCILIATION.
