# Publication and Specialized-Visa Strategy Context

This file summarizes strategic principles for publication planning relevant to the dissertation decision. It is research-portfolio planning, not legal advice.

## Established principles

- Optimize for coherent, high-quality research contributions rather than publication count alone.
- Prefer dissertation programs that can decompose into multiple substantial papers without becoming fragmented.
- Consider time to first publishable unit and the probability of sustained publication output.
- Venue fit matters more than chasing prestige when the technical contribution does not belong in that community.
- Citations, independent adoption, datasets/software use, invited talks, external recognition, and legitimate reviewing/judging activity can be more informative than venue metrics alone.
- Journal impact factor is contextual evidence, not a direct measure of the significance of an individual paper.
- Publication strategy should support a recognizable scholarly identity.

## Evidence assets to preserve over time

- acceptance and publication records
- citation trajectories
- software/dataset adoption
- external replications
- invited talks
- independent expert discussion
- clinical or operational pilots
- reviewing/judging records
- downstream collaborations
